Help and answers

Playing

How do I play?

Drag left and right to steer your crowd through the gates. Each gate does maths to your crowd (+, −, ×, ÷), so pick the one that makes it biggest. Avoid saws and enemy squads, then beat the boss castle at the end.

What are Duels?

In a duel a rival runs the same level beside you. You both meet at the end and the bigger crowd wins. If it's within 2%, you play a quick Showdown minigame to decide it. Wins earn trophies that move you up the leagues, from Wood to Champion, and unlock rewards on the Trophy Road.

What is Tower Clash?

A puzzle mode: climb a tower room by room from the bottom, beating enemies whose power is smaller than yours and adding it to your own. Plan your route, grab potions late for a bigger boost and skip the traps. In a Tower duel, reaching the top first gives a 10% bonus.

The game is slow or stutters on my phone.

Set Settings → Graphics to LOW, or leave it on AUTO: the game lowers its own detail when your phone is struggling and remembers that setting. Closing other apps also helps.

Purchases

I bought the VIP Pack. How do I get it back on a new phone?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account, open the game and tap Settings → Restore Purchases. This restores the VIP Pack (no ads plus its bonus items) and the Starter Pack.

Can gems be restored?

No. Gems are used up when you spend them and your balance is saved only on your phone, so unspent gems are lost if you delete the game or reset progress. Items that need a purchase (VIP, Starter Pack) can be restored.

I paid but didn't get my item.

Close and reopen the game while online: purchases still being processed are delivered when the store confirms them. If it still hasn't arrived, use Restore Purchases in Settings, or ask the store you bought from for help.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. iPhone: reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history.

Ads & progress

How do I remove ads?

Buy the VIP Pack in the shop. It removes every ad that plays without your choice and gives you bonus coins and items. You can still watch optional reward ads for extra coins if you want to.

How do I change my ad privacy choice?

Where the law requires a choice (for example in the EU and UK), open Settings → Ad Privacy in the game. On iPhone you can also change tracking permission in your phone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Why is there a break when I play offline?

The game works without internet. Ad breaks can't load offline, so a short offline break takes their place, and the break you missed is shown once you're back online. The VIP Pack removes all ad breaks, online and offline.

I lost my progress.

Progress is saved only on your phone. There are no accounts, so deleting the app or clearing its data erases it, and it can't be recovered from our side. Purchases can still be restored as described above.
