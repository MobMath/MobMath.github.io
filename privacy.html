Mob Math: Gate Rush
Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

In short: Mob Math: Gate Rush has no accounts and the developer runs no servers, so we do not collect your personal information ourselves. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show those ads, as explained below. The optional VIP Pack is sold through the App Store or Google Play.

Information we collect

None directly. The game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not run analytics.

Logs on your device

The game keeps a short log of your recent runs and of any errors, on your device only, to help it recover from problems. It is never sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To show ads, cap how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game may collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking)
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • Information about the ads you see and tap, and how the app performs (for example launch time and crashes)

Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area and the UK, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad Privacy. On iPhone you can also allow or deny tracking, and change it later in your phone's Settings. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences.

Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases: the VIP Pack, the Starter Pack and gem packs. They are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). They process the payment; we never see your card or account details. The store tells the game only whether a purchase was completed, so the item can be delivered. The VIP Pack and the Starter Pack can be restored on a new device with Restore Purchases. Gems are kept only on your device, so gems you have not spent cannot be restored if you delete the game.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (level, coins, gems, skins, hats, trails, trophies and settings) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Delete it any time with Settings → Reset Progress or by uninstalling the app. A VIP Pack you bought can be restored with Restore Purchases.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly allow ad personalization for children.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support.reign@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
